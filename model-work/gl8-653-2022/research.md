# GL8 ES Lu Zun 653T — 2022 reference dossier

This dossier describes a model-production reference, not verified rental-fleet equipment.

## Primary evidence

1. C-IASI official test report: https://www.ciasi.org.cn/uploads/files/e6f45547c9d28d06d333719f56e5acd1.pdf
   Page 2 identifies 2022 GL8 ES Lu Zun 653T Comfort, Luxury and Flagship test variants. Visual inspection of the original report confirms SGM6522UBB2 for all three listed variants; the apparent trailing P in extracted text came from the next page and was not part of the model code. The variants must not be treated as one common equipment package. The agency's page also explicitly warns that the base test configuration did not have AEB.
   Official result page: https://www.ciasi.org.cn/products/show-783.html
2. Manufacturer-supplied launch announcement hosted by Gasgoo, dated 2021-09-13: https://auto.gasgoo.com/news/202109/13I70272527C107.shtml
   It describes the 2022 GL8 ES Lu Zun launch. Published exterior dimensions are 5219 × 1878 × 1799 mm, with a 3088 mm wheelbase. It lists Tourmaline Brown and Desert Gold as additional paint choices, and a 2.0T/9-speed/48V powertrain. The article explicitly marks itself as company-supplied content. These are reference-family facts; they do not identify this customer's specific trim, registration year, paint code or retrofits.

## Modeling decisions

Use the 2022 ES Lu Zun body as the reference target. Retain variant uncertainty in the source record. Do not use the 2022-August refreshed GL8 family as proof of a 2022-model-year front fascia. A date in a launch title is not a sufficient year/trim match.

Lux3D China was selected under the user's delegated account-setup instruction. No usable Key, live quote, paid-generation approval or submitted task is present. Browser interaction repeatedly timed out. Setup/login is pending; never invent a task ID or a zero-cost quote.

Production parts: exterior body shell, glazing, wheels/tyres, grille/headlamps/tail lamps and exterior trim are independent editable parts. Interior/seating is excluded until the actual version/layout is verified. Lux3D creates reference-guided source parts after a real quote is approved; Blender performs scale checks, mesh correction, part assembly, materials, lighting and embedded GLB export. Repeated wheels reuse a single verified design; an unknown wheel design is not guessed as fleet equipment.

Acceptance requires matching silhouette and front/rear/side views, body-only width measurement (excluding mirrors where applicable), wheelbase, actual trim details, material resources and absence of invented seating. A dimensional reference fixture is not a completed vehicle mesh. A photo or AI-rendered image is not a 3D deliverable.
